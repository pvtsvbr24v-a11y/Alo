Shipping & Returns

Shipping Rates & Times

Orders are hand-packed and ship within 2 business days. Standard shipping is free on orders over $50; otherwise a flat $5 applies at checkout.

  • United States: 4–7 business days
  • Canada: 6–10 business days
  • International: 7–14 business days

You'll receive a shipping confirmation as soon as your order leaves our studio.

Returns & Exchanges

We want you to love what you carry. If something isn't right, unused items in their original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to your original payment method.

  • Contact us with your order number to start a return.
  • Pack the item securely in its original packaging.
  • Refunds are issued once the item is received and inspected, usually within 5 business days.

Exchanges (say, a different phone model) follow the same process — just let us know what you'd like instead.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, contact us within 7 days with a photo and your order number, and we'll make it right with a replacement or full refund — no return shipping required.

International Orders

International customers are responsible for any customs duties or import taxes charged by their country. These are not included in the checkout total.

Questions?

Reach out any time through our contact page — we typically reply within one business day.
